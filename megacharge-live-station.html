Questions people ask us

What exactly do I own?

The charging station itself, registered in your name, and the earnings from the electricity it sells. [Confirm legal wording: ownership of the specific unit vs. partnership interest.]

How is my payout worked out?

From the units (kWh) your station sells each month, at the site’s charging price, after upkeep costs. [Confirm formula / revenue share.] Every unit is visible in your dashboard.

Is the payout guaranteed?

No. It’s a target. If drivers use the station more, you earn more; if they use it less, you earn less. Tariff changes and power cuts also affect it.
